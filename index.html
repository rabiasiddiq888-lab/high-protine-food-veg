Best Vegetarian Protein Foods

A varied vegetarian diet can include protein from beans, lentils, soy foods, dairy products, nuts, and seeds.

Lentils & Beans

Lentils, kidney beans, black beans, and chickpeas provide protein and fiber.

Tofu & Tempeh

Soy-based foods are versatile choices for stir-fries, curries, and salads.

Greek Yogurt & Cottage Cheese

Dairy foods can add protein to breakfast, snacks, and savory meals.

Nuts & Seeds

Peanuts, almonds, chia seeds, pumpkin seeds, and hemp seeds can add protein and healthy fats.

Milk & Soy Milk

Milk and fortified soy milk can be useful additions to smoothies, oats, or drinks.

Whole Grains

Oats, quinoa, and whole-grain breads contribute protein alongside other nutrients.

Easy Vegetarian Meal Ideas

  • Breakfast: oatmeal with milk or soy milk, nuts, and seeds.
  • Lunch: chickpea salad with vegetables and whole-grain bread.
  • Dinner: lentil curry with rice or whole-wheat roti.
  • Snack: plain yogurt, roasted chickpeas, or a handful of nuts.
  • Quick meal: tofu and vegetable stir-fry.

Helpful Tips

  • Include a source of protein in meals and snacks that suit your needs.
  • Eat a variety of legumes, soy foods, grains, nuts, and seeds.
  • Choose foods you enjoy and can afford consistently.
  • Protein needs vary by age, body size, activity level, and health status.
